About Me

I’m Ghosbakhsh, son of Ellahibakhsh — a passionate creator from Balgater District Kech with a growing love for technology and design. My journey began at Government Boys High School, Hoshab, where I completed my matriculation. From there, my curiosity for the digital world pushed me forward. I completed my computer basics in Turbat, which opened the door to bigger dreams. Later, I trained in Web Development and Graphic Designing through Muslim Hands and Informatics Quetta, where I learned how to turn ideas into modern, functional, and visually striking digital experiences. Today, I blend creativity with clean development to craft bold, meaningful projects — combining the worlds of code and design to tell powerful stories.
