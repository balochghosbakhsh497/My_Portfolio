The Storyteller

Born and raised in the heart of Gwadar, Balochistan, I am Sakul Jan, a visual storyteller dedicated to capturing the raw essence of my homeland.

My lens seeks the untold stories hidden in the rugged landscapes, the vibrant culture, and the quiet resilience of life in Balochistan. Through light and shadow, I aim to preserve moments that speak of history, beauty, and the human spirit.
