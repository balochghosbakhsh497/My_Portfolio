Kech Museum Turbat

A cultural home for archaeology, history, and heritage of the Kech region in Balochistan. Discover artifacts, oral traditions, and craftsmanship shaped by desert and sea.

Plan Your Visit Explore Galleries

Welcome

Kech Museum preserves the material culture of Turbat and Makran—ceramics, textiles, tools, and stories that connect the region’s past to its living traditions.

Archaeology

Excavations and surveys from Makran reveal settlement patterns, trade, and craftsmanship across centuries.

History

Documents and oral histories trace governance, migration, and everyday life from ancient to modern eras.

Culture

Weaving, music, and language reflect resilient identities shaped by landscape and exchange.

Heritage

Conservation and community programs safeguard artifacts and knowledge for future generations.
