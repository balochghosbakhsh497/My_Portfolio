About the Museum

Kech Museum Turbat curates the rich material culture of the Kech region—connecting archaeology and living heritage through research, exhibitions, and community partnerships.

History

Origins, growth, and the museum’s commitment to preservation

Founding

Established to safeguard regional artifacts and oral histories, Kech Museum Turbat grew from local initiatives to a dedicated cultural institution.

Collections

Archaeological finds, textiles, tools, and archival materials document life across Makran—from coastal trade to inland crafts.

Research

Partnerships with universities and community scholars guide documentation, conservation, and exhibition design rooted in local knowledge.

Cultural Importance

Why Kech matters in Balochistan and beyond

Identity

Heritage practices—weaving, music, storytelling—anchor community identities and connect generations.

Education

Exhibits and programs encourage learning through artifacts, oral histories, and hands-on activities for all ages.

Conservation

The museum supports ethical conservation and documentation, ensuring artifacts remain accessible and meaningful.
